---
name: Self-likes are not a product feature
description: Why members must not like their own posts or comments.
---

Members must not be able to like their own posts or comments.

**Why:** The owner said self-liking was enabled only to check alerts and badging,
not as an intended member feature.

**How to apply:** Preserve this restriction in both client interactions and
server authorization when changing reactions. Do not re-enable self-likes for
notification checks.
