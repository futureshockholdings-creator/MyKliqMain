---
name: Kliq invite card scope
description: Intentional difference between Kliq capacity messaging and the early-growth invitation cutoff.
---

Kliqs offer 28 friends. The headlines invitation card should communicate that capacity but disappear once a user has 10 friends.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested different numbers for capacity messaging and the invitation cutoff. Ten is an onboarding cutoff, not the Kliq limit.

**How to apply:** Do not change the disappearance threshold to match the displayed capacity, or reduce capacity messaging to match the threshold.
